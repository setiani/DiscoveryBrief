# DiscoveryBrief - 3-minute English demo

Narration: synthetic English voice (Microsoft Zira Desktop).
Footage: actual local application; public-company live result with synthetic meeting context.
Research wait omitted. Separate context-only scene is an identified synthetic replay.

## 0:00 - Start with context

DiscoveryBrief helps pre-sales teams prepare for better discovery meetings. It connects recent company research, customer requirements, and questions you can actually use, with evidence you can inspect.

## 0:14 - The meeting-preparation problem

Before a customer meeting, useful information is scattered across company announcements, documents, and notes. A convincing summary is not enough. You need to know what changed, what the customer explicitly asked for, and what still needs confirmation.

## 0:32 - Give the meeting its context

Here is the real application. We enter OpenAI and its official domain, choose a meeting time, and request an English brief. This demonstration uses a public company with fictional meeting context. The sample RFP asks for single sign-on and document repository integration. Private notes, participant names, and the RFP are kept out of public search queries.

## 1:00 - Research, then verify

Tavily searches the public web and extracts selected pages. Every live model request goes through Bifrost. Separate Planner, Analyst, Verifier, Composer, and Auditor steps organize the work. Application code checks dates, references, and exact quotations. The research wait is omitted from this edited demonstration.

## 1:20 - A brief you can inspect

The completed live result brings the meeting context and research together. Recent developments are separate from RFP requirements, questions, hypotheses, and unknowns. The visible quality label describes available coverage. It is not an accuracy score. A hypothesis remains something to confirm, rather than an assertion about the customer.

## 1:44 - Follow a finding to its evidence

Open the evidence section to inspect a literal source quotation and its link. Event dates help explain why a development belongs in the research window. RFP requirements also retain quotations from the submitted text. This lets a pre-sales engineer check the basis of a statement before repeating it in a customer conversation.

## 2:10 - Turn context into questions

The priority questions turn preparation into a meeting agenda. They connect the supplied requirements and available evidence to topics for clarification. The usage view records operational details, including retrieval and model calls. Unknown cost or usage stays unknown, instead of appearing as zero.

## 2:28 - Show uncertainty honestly

This separate example is a clearly labelled synthetic replay. When no public development is verified, the result is context only. DiscoveryBrief can still organize user-provided context and questions, while making the missing evidence visible.

## 2:44 - Take the brief into the meeting

Export a one-page PDF and keep the detailed evidence separately. DiscoveryBrief is a working local prototype, with independent human quality evaluation still pending. Prepare with context. Ask with purpose. Keep the evidence within reach.
